Wound infection signs: redness, swelling, warmth, pus, fever above 38C. Treatment: clean with saline, apply antiseptic, cover with sterile bandage. Medicine: Amoxicillin 500mg 3x daily for 7 days. Always consult a doctor for deep wounds.

Fever: temperature above 38C. Causes: viral or bacterial infection. Medicine: Paracetamol 500mg every 6 hours. Ibuprofen 400mg every 8 hours with food. See doctor if fever lasts more than 3 days or goes above 40C.

Common cold: runny nose, sore throat, mild cough. Treatment: rest, fluids, honey-lemon tea. Medicine: Cetirizine 10mg once daily for runny nose. Strepsils lozenges for sore throat. Steam inhalation helps with congestion.

Diabetes management: check blood sugar daily. Normal fasting range: 70-100 mg/dL. Medicine: Metformin 500mg twice daily with meals. Monitor for hypoglycemia symptoms: shaking, sweating, confusion. Eat small frequent meals.

High blood pressure: normal is below 120/80 mmHg. Medicine: Amlodipine 5mg once daily. Reduce salt intake. Exercise 30 minutes daily. Avoid stress and smoking.

Burn treatment: cool running water for 20 minutes. Do not use ice, butter, or toothpaste. Cover with clean bandage. Medicine: Silverex cream for minor burns. Take Paracetamol for pain. See doctor if burn is larger than palm size or on face/hands.

Skin rash or allergy: red itchy patches on skin. Medicine: Cetirizine 10mg once daily. Hydrocortisone cream applied twice daily. Avoid allergens. See doctor if rash spreads rapidly.

Stomach pain or gastritis: burning pain in stomach, nausea, bloating. Medicine: Omeprazole 20mg once daily before breakfast. Antacid syrup for quick relief. Avoid spicy and oily food. Eat smaller portions.

Diarrhea: loose stools more than 3 times a day. Treatment: ORS (oral rehydration salts) after every loose stool. Medicine: Metronidazole 400mg 3x daily if bacterial. Avoid dairy and fatty foods. Stay hydrated.

Urinary tract infection: burning during urination, frequent urge, cloudy urine. Medicine: Ciprofloxacin 500mg twice daily for 5 days. Drink lots of water. Cranberry juice helps. See doctor for urine culture test.

Headache and migraine: throbbing pain usually on one side. Medicine: Paracetamol 500mg for mild headache. Ibuprofen 400mg for moderate pain. Rest in dark quiet room. Apply cold or warm compress. See doctor if headaches are frequent.

Conjunctivitis (eye infection): red eyes, discharge, itching. Medicine: Ciprofloxacin eye drops 4x daily. Chloramphenicol eye ointment at night. Do not touch eyes with dirty hands. Change pillowcase daily.

Cough: dry cough or productive cough. Medicine: Dextromethorphan syrup for dry cough. Ambroxol syrup for productive cough with phlegm. Steam inhalation twice daily. Honey with ginger helps naturally.

Toothache: pain in tooth or gum area. Medicine: Ibuprofen 400mg for pain. Clove oil applied on affected tooth. See dentist as soon as possible. Rinse with warm salt water.

Back pain: lower or upper back pain. Medicine: Ibuprofen 400mg 3x daily with food. Diclofenac gel applied on painful area. Rest but avoid complete bed rest. Gentle stretching exercises help.

Anxiety and stress: feeling worried, fast heartbeat, difficulty sleeping. Advice: Practice deep breathing exercises. 7-8 hours sleep is essential. Reduce caffeine. Talk to someone you trust. See doctor if it affects daily life.

Anemia: tiredness, pale skin, shortness of breath. Medicine: Ferrous sulfate 200mg once daily with vitamin C for better absorption. Eat iron-rich foods: red meat, lentils, spinach, dates. Blood test needed for confirmation.

Chest pain warning: chest pain can be a sign of heart attack. Emergency symptoms: chest tightness, pain spreading to arm or jaw, sweating, shortness of breath. CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES IMMEDIATELY. Do not drive yourself to hospital.
